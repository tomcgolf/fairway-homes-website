Privacy & Disclosures

A plain-language summary of how Fairway Homes handles information and what to expect when working with Fairway Homes.

Information Submitted Through Forms

Information you share through a Fairway Homes form, or by phone, text, or email, is used to respond to your inquiry and to follow up about properties or purchasing options that may fit what you're looking for.

How Fairway Homes May Use Contact Information

Fairway Homes may use the phone number, email address, or other contact information you provide to follow up with you directly about your inquiry, available properties, or a specific property you've asked about.

No Sale or Public Disclosure of Personal Application Information

Fairway Homes does not sell the personal information you submit, and does not publicly disclose the details of individual buyer or seller inquiries.

No Guaranteed Approval or Financing

Submitting a form or contacting Fairway Homes does not guarantee approval, financing, or the availability of any property. Where owner financing is offered, it depends on the property and on buyer qualification.

Property Availability and Terms May Change

Property availability, pricing, and purchasing options can change at any time without notice.

Not a Real Estate Brokerage

Fairway Homes is not a real estate brokerage and does not represent buyers or sellers as an agent.

Owner Financing

Owner financing is available only on selected properties, and is subject to qualification and to terms agreed to by Fairway Homes and the buyer.

About This Page

This page is a plain-language summary provided for general information. It is not legal advice, and Fairway Homes does not represent that this page has been reviewed or verified for legal compliance in any jurisdiction.
