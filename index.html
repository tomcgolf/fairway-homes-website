About

About Fairway Homes

Fairway Homes buys residential properties and makes selected homes it owns available for purchase throughout North Carolina and South Carolina. Some of those homes offer owner-financing options, giving buyers more ways to purchase a home directly. Fairway Homes is not a real estate brokerage and does not represent buyers or sellers as an agent.
